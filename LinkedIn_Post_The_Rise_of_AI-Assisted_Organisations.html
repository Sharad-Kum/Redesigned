The Rise of AI-Assisted Organizations

Early AI-assisted organizations are demonstrating differentiation not just in Delivery, but across Solutions and Go-to-Market. This is not just automation — AI is now enabling critical layers across the entire value chain.

The advantage will lie in how effectively organizations integrate AI-assisted Delivery, Solutions and GTM — speed from problem identification to market-ready solution will be the differentiator.

AI is enabling the entire value chain — not just automating it. In Delivery, it augments the engineering lifecycle from code to production, with tools like GitHub Copilot, Cursor IDE, Mabl, Dynatrace and Logic Monitor now embedded across coding, testing and diagnostics. Across Go-to-Market, AI sharpens messaging, accelerates content and drives insight-led outreach, with platforms like HubSpot, Salesforce and Jasper AI enhancing the GTM motion. In Solutions, faster problem framing, architecture pattern recognition and knowledge synthesis — through tools like Notion AI, Confluence and ChatGPT — are enabling contextual thinking at scale.
