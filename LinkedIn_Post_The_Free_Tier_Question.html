The Free-Tier Question

The absence of responses from LinkedIn is entirely understandable — platform design, recommendation systems, network effects, and analytics interpretation rarely have simple or universally accepted answers.

As a deliberate choice, I remain on the free tier — not because of cost, but because direct accessibility to the professional community is closer to the value proposition that first attracted me to the platform.

Returning to the first inquiry: Impressions vs Members Reached
What a widening gap could mean
Repeated Exposure
Does a higher impressions-to-reach ratio simply indicate repeated exposure to the same audience?
New vs Existing Audience
What determines whether content is resurfaced to the same audience versus introduced to a new one?
Depth vs Breadth
Is the objective depth of visibility, breadth of visibility, or some balance of the two?

Do subscription tiers, creator features, account attributes, or network structure influence visibility? Which metric determines whether content qualifies for a second, third, or fourth wave of distribution?

Perhaps understanding visibility begins with understanding the relationship between these two numbers.
