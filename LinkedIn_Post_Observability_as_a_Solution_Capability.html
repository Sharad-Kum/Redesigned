Observability as a Solution Capability

Monitoring tells us something broke. Observability tells us why, before it is felt. This distinction is quietly reshaping how solutions are built, positioned, and trusted in the market.

Most organizations still treat observability as an engineering concern. The ones pulling ahead are treating it as a solution capability — with direct impact on delivery confidence, client trust, and go-to-market positioning.

Commonwealth Bank – Australia
1,100+ services under real-time telemetry
CommSec deployments 2× faster
23% digital engagement growth — reliability became the brand
BT Group – UK
16 monitoring platforms consolidated into one unified observability stack
Issue detection reduced from 30 minutes to near real-time
Projected cumulative savings of £28M by 2027
Roadmap to self-healing, automated remediation systems
Netflix – USA
Observability built into architecture — not bolted on
Anomaly detection before customer impact
Continuous releases across 190+ markets — speed made sustainable through reliability

Tooling mindset
