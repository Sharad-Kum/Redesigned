AI Sits Inside the H (How)

AI agents are now acting on real systems, not just answering questions.

So where does AI belong? Inside the H (How), not in place of it.

The previous edition made GTM the Sun. This one asks: who stays in the driver's seat?

The H is the How: the execution layer that holds scope, risk, teams, SLAs, decisions and delivery. AI shapes the speed and quality of that execution: faster decisions, automated actions, scaled teams. Yet it does not own the mandate.

An H without AI will struggle to keep pace. An H handed over to AI has outrun its own controls.

Every function has a different H, so every function will use AI differently.

The common point is control: what AI can do, what data it can touch, where it can act, and where human sign-off remains mandatory. One AI strategy. Many functional Hs. Clear boundaries around authority.

AI inside the H accelerates execution. AI replacing the H hands control away.

Too far into the H
Klarna
Klarna shows AI moving too far into the H. In 2024, its AI assistant was reported to do the work of 700 agents; by 2025, it was rebuilding human capacity amid quality concerns.
Accountability stays
JPMorgan
JPMorgan shows another model. Its LLM Suite is used across the business, yet accountability stays with employees, especially where AI touches customers, as its executives have said.

Speed has always been easy to buy. The question is who decides what AI touches, and when.
