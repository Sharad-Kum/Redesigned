The Sun Doesn't Orbit

GTM is like the Sun. Every other function is a planet — orbiting around it while still turning on its own axis, adjusting as required.

Edition 4 was about where the flow starts in the sequence. This one extends that: GTM isn't just where the flow starts — it's also what everything else moves around.

A planet can wobble on its own axis. The Sun doesn't answer to the wobble.

Vision sets GTM's strategy. GTM in turn shapes structure, geography, solution, talent and delivery — every other function's outcomes trace back to it, directly or indirectly.

Other functions can react to a signal — a complaint, a competitor, a weak quarter. GTM moves on a pattern: repeated, structural, hard to unsee.

Exemplar
Samsung
Samsung didn't fold the Galaxy Note into the Ultra tier because of one weak launch. It had already begun moving the Note experience into the S series with the S21 Ultra in 2021. In 2022, the S22 Ultra brought the built-in S Pen and other Note characteristics into the S line. The product architecture had become a pattern, not an incident.

Ask any function why it changed this year, and the answer is usually a signal. Ask GTM the same question — and if the answer isn't a pattern, that's worth asking twice.
